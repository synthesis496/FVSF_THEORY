## Overview

This repository hosts the full technical manuscript and supporting derivations for the FVS framework. The central idea is that a small set of structural constants — most notably the heterotic lattice dimensions and the golden ratio φ — is used to generate a family of numerical relations for observable quantities such as:

- α⁻¹ = 248·φ⁻¹ − 16 − φ⁻³ = 137.0364
- Ω_DM/Ω_m = (248 − 36)/248 = 85.48%
- E_recoil = m_e c² / φ^(3/2) = 248.3 keV
- m_p/m_e = 512φ² + 496 = 1836.43
- Λ = φ⁻⁵⁸¹ = 3.78 × 10⁻¹²²
- r = φ⁻¹² = 0.00311

The theory is organized around a four-pillar mechanism:

1. Golden ratio scaling
2. Alternating parity structure
3. Gauge-commutator invariance
4. Zero-sum parity integration

These pillars are used to motivate a unified temporal and geometric description spanning quantum, macro, and cosmological scales.

## Abstract

We develop a geometric field theory, Fibo Vortex Spiral Space (FVS) Field Theory, in which structural constants related to E8×E8 and SO(32) heterotic lattice geometry, together with the golden ratio φ, determine a set of Standard Model and cosmological relations. The theory is constructed as a unified temporal field formalism in which Fibonacci-like scaling, parity alternation, and geometric cancellation mechanisms define the dynamics across quantum, macroscopic, and cosmological regimes.

## Research emphasis

### 1. Core mathematical framework

The manuscript develops a formal theory built from:

- the D16⁺ lattice base structure
- the dual heterotic gauge structures SO(32) and E8×E8
- a fractal Fibonacci time field T_Fib(t)
- parity-based cancellation constraints
- dissipative healing dynamics as a stability mechanism

### 2. Numerical derivations

A central part of the work is the derivation of numerical relations for key observables, including:

- fine-structure constant
- dark matter fraction
- recoil energy scale
- proton-to-electron mass ratio
- weak mixing angle
- scalar spectral index
- cosmological constant
- tensor-to-scalar ratio

### 3. Falsifiable predictions

The repository also includes a dedicated section containing a series of explicit predictions intended for empirical testing in cosmology, dark matter searches, and high-energy physics.
